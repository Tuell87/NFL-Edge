How to use this release

Game scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard. Player odds come from a separately scheduled FanDuel odds snapshot supplied through The Odds API. A feed timestamp indicates when the snapshot was collected, not a guarantee that the underlying bookmaker price is unchanged. The odds calculator requires your own probability estimate; it is not an NFL model.
