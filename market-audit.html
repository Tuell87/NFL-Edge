Method

Observed past-game over frequency with Beta(2,2) smoothing and at least five historical games. Data must have recent feed timestamps, an unambiguous future matchup, and a single Over market. This is not adjusted for injuries, snap share, opponent defense or game conditions. Prices can change at any time. No positive-EV claims or parlays are generated.
