What this measures

Brier score is average squared probability error (lower is better). Each test uses only that player's earlier games, with at least five previous observations and smoothing toward 50%. The comparison baseline always predicts 50%. Thresholds are illustrative and not current FanDuel lines.

Player availability, injuries, upcoming opponents and matchup conditions are not verified or adjusted. Historical samples may be incomplete. No bets or parlays are recommended from this test.
