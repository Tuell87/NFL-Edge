Interpretation

Lower Brier score is better. This evaluates the final quarter of available historical game dates, using only each player's earlier games to calculate the estimate. Two references are shown: a constant 50% prediction and a smoothed pooled over rate calculated exclusively from dates before the holdout cutoff. Lower Brier is better; beating either benchmark does not prove a betting edge. At least 30 scored observations spanning four held-out dates per metric are required for a preliminary label. No label makes a prediction betting-ready. These test thresholds are illustrative and are not FanDuel lines. The historical data can be incomplete; injury, roster, matchup, and market adjustments are not modeled.
