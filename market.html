How to interpret this

The no-vig estimate normalizes the two implied probabilities to sum to 100%. It is a simple proportional-vig method, not necessarily the true fair probability. Compare this baseline with a separately calibrated model before evaluating betting value.

Do not combine prices from different sportsbooks or different prop thresholds. Enter odds manually; this page does not fetch or verify live FanDuel prices, place bets, or save information.
