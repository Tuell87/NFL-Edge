Limitations

Lower mean absolute error (MAE) is better. Positive improvement means the adjusted method performed better on the paired sample. A positive value does not prove it will work in future games. The 95% event-cluster bootstrap interval resamples complete games, but cannot correct for incomplete historical collection, nonrandom seasons, or future distribution changes.
